# Budget Lab

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Gen Z users managing a limited ₹10,000 budget over 30 days, as specified in the challenge.

## Product Purpose

Connect confirmed cash, spending, future bills, subscriptions, savings, and financial
health so users understand the consequences of their month. My Month is the editable overview. What If compares isolated proposals with the current
month, then supports idempotent Apply, Discard, and Undo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React, strict TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind. The tested pure engine is the single
source for calculated values. All money uses integer paise. Sample data must be
labelled. Start fresh and Reset sample are required. Committed changes persist locally using a versioned, validated browser store with
backup recovery. Draft previews stay temporary. Accounts, banking connections,
external services, and deployment are outside this request.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic controls, explicit labels, keyboard navigation, visible focus, and responsive
layouts follow the challenge rubric. INR amounts must be legible at mobile widths.
